## Email the maintainer

Write to **[support@getfilmcraft.org](mailto:support@getfilmcraft.org)**. This address is for questions about getfilmcraft.org, corrections to a guide, broken links and feedback about the website. You do not need an account or a contact form to send a message.

We aim to reply within **3–5 business days**. This is a target, not a guaranteed response time; messages requiring investigation may take longer. If you follow up, reply in the same email thread so the original page address and context stay together.

## What to include

- The address of the page you are asking about.
- The instruction, link or sentence that needs attention.
- Your operating system and FilmCraft version when the issue concerns a workflow.
- What you expected to happen and what actually happened.

For an article correction, a link to the relevant official documentation or release note is especially useful. If a download link is broken, include the operating system and package name you selected. Please do not send passwords, account tokens, private project files or footage you cannot share.

## Website help and software support

getfilmcraft.org is an independent guide. Its maintainer can address the website's content and links, but cannot issue FilmCraft software fixes or speak on behalf of its developers. Reproducible application bugs can be reported through the [official FilmCraft issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft/issues), after checking whether the issue is already known.

If the problem is that our instructions do not match your installed version, email us even if you also report it upstream. Those are different problems: the software project handles the application, while this site can correct the guide.

For information about the maintainer's role and how articles are prepared, read [About getfilmcraft.org](/about). For information about website data handling, read the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
