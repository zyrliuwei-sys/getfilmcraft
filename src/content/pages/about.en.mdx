## What this site does

getfilmcraft.org is an English-language guide for people learning to use the FilmCraft video editor. It brings together download guidance, browser setup instructions and practical editing workflows. The aim is to help you complete a task, understand the choices involved and recognize where a feature or workflow has limits.

The site links to the FilmCraft project's own distribution channels. It does not develop FilmCraft, publish its installers or provide official technical support for the application. Before installing a build, check its upstream release notes and the files offered by the publisher.

## Who maintains it

This site is maintained by an independent developer. Questions about the website, corrections and requests for clearer instructions can be sent to [support@getfilmcraft.org](mailto:support@getfilmcraft.org). No affiliation with the software's developers is implied by the site name.

## Where the content comes from

Guides are prepared from the project's public documentation, release information and, where useful, the relevant interface or source-code definitions. Articles link to the references supporting their steps. Version-specific guides identify the release they were checked against; a newer build can change a menu label or available setting.

Explanatory editing exercises are examples, not measured performance tests. Product screenshots illustrate the interface, and diagrams are labeled as illustrations. The site does not present invented benchmarks, measured speedups or production-readiness claims as its own testing. Recommendations are framed around a workflow you can check with your own media.

The download pages retrieve the latest stable release version, publication date and asset names from GitHub. A verified local snapshot is available if that request fails. For the authoritative current release, follow the official release link on the download page.

If you find an error, send the page address, your FilmCraft version and the step that differs. Corrections should explain the limitation as clearly as the successful workflow. The [guides index](/guides) is the starting point for articles, and the [contact page](/contact) explains how to reach the maintainer.
